Milestone 15 — ADINA-like full planar frame diagnostic

This milestone replaces the scalar bending-only model with a full planar
frame formulation using three nodal DOFs [ux, uy, theta_z]. Both links are
assembled into one global mass/stiffness system. At joint 2 the global x-y
translations are shared; the two link rotations remain independent, matching
the wording in Chapter 5. Base translations are fixed while base rotation is
free.

The goal is diagnostic, not an exact ADINA reproduction. ADINA's historical
element library, constraint implementation, and any user-defined offsets or
joint/transmission details are not available from the thesis alone.
