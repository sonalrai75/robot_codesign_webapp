match the four published final performance values?

- Link 1 is nearly uniform with mild widening.
- Link 2 is oscillatory, not a smooth taper.

Topology-constrained test:
Enforced link 1 monotonically widening toward joint and link 2 monotonically
tapering toward tip, qualitatively matching Figure 5-6.

Best result:
outputs = [0.0065441361, 0.0259709865, 89.6690654, 259.970858]
errors (%) = [-0.8464, -7.5766, +24.5404, -6.9206]

Conclusion:
Under the CURRENT reconstructed FE model, the thesis-like visual topology does
not emerge naturally while reproducing all four final performance values.
This is evidence that the remaining FE/modeling discrepancy matters. It is not
evidence that the thesis shape is inconsistent, because the current model
already fails to reproduce the published intermediate frequencies exactly.
