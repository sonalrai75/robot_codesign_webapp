Milestone 16 — Utility of the redundant design space

Primary performance target:
Hmin=0.0066, Hmax=0.0281, f1=72.0 Hz, f2=279.3 Hz

A target-matching 20-width design was used.  The local log-performance
Jacobian is 4 x 20, giving a 16-dimensional numerical null space.

Initial singular values:
[0.795349 0.45807  0.245605 0.181526]

Secondary objective demonstrated: minimize structural mass while retaining
the four primary performance quantities.

Baseline:
performance = [6.59999393e-03 2.81000201e-02 7.20000433e+01 2.79299145e+02]
mass = 1.07573009 kg

After nonlinear null-space continuation with SVD correction:
performance = [6.60000000e-03 2.81000000e-02 7.19999999e+01 2.79300000e+02]
relative errors (%) = [-1.11022e-14  0.00000e+00 -1.70150e-07 -8.88130e-09]
mass = 0.93667634 kg
mass reduction = 12.9265 %

Interpretation:
This is a direct numerical demonstration that the locally redundant
directions are useful design freedom.  Motion is projected into the null
space of the current performance Jacobian, then the full nonlinear model is
re-evaluated and corrected back to the target manifold.  The null space is
recomputed after each accepted move.

This is a methodological demonstration in the reconstructed model, not a
claim that the resulting shape is the historical 1993 final design.
