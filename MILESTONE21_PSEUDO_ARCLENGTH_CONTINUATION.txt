Method:
At each target-matching point, the 16-D null space of the four-output Jacobian was
computed. A tangent direction was chosen as the projected gradient direction that
decreases sigma_min. To obtain a locally one-dimensional continuation path, the
15 null directions perpendicular to that tangent were frozen. A predictor was taken
along the tangent, followed by a 20-equation corrector consisting of the four primary
performance equations, 15 transverse slice equations, and one arclength hyperplane
condition. This is a pseudo-arclength-style continuation on a one-dimensional slice
of the high-dimensional constant-performance manifold.

Interpretation:
A decreasing sigma_min together with a rapidly increasing condition number is evidence
of approach to a Jacobian rank-loss region. It is not by itself proof of a bifurcation.
If sigma_min can be made arbitrarily small and continuation can pass the singular point,
the next test is to branch-probe on the other side and determine whether distinct
target-matching solution families emerge.
