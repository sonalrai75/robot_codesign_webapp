Milestone 13 — endpoint/joint mass and joint-rotation diagnostic

Published intermediate frequencies: 48, 248 Hz
Current free-relative-rotation model: 58.6346, 184.4026 Hz

Key result:
Adding positive translational or rotary inertia cannot raise an ordered natural
frequency for fixed K; it only increases M. Therefore an omitted gripper/payload
rotary inertia cannot by itself explain why the current second mode is too low
(184.4 Hz versus 248 Hz).

Explicit rotary-inertia sweep confirms both modes move downward.

Joint-2 relative rotation test:
- Thesis wording says the two arms share displacement at joint 2 but are free
  to rotate relative to each other.
- Current model implements that condition.
- Artificially locking the two rotations gives:
  25.6058, 92.5766 Hz

Thus changing the joint rotational constraint has a large modal effect, but the
locked model is not the boundary condition stated in the thesis.

Conclusion:
The endpoint mass representation alone is not a viable explanation of the
48/248-Hz discrepancy. The remaining candidates are FE formulation/section
properties, configuration-dependent 2-D assembly details, or another historical
modeling convention not explicitly documented in the thesis.
